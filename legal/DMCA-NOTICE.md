Zyraxon Sovereign License - X Edition (ZSL-X) is the licence that governs this
software. It is not an open-source licence. It permits free use, free modification
and contribution, and it deliberately does not permit resale, republication as the
licensee's own product, or removal of the origin notice.

This document explains how enforcement notices are handled. It is a statement of
procedure, not legal advice.

## 1. What ZSL-X protects

The licence is enforced through the copyright holder, `onelpawarai`. The following

- Selling, sublicensing, renting, leasing or otherwise commercialising the

- Removing, obscuring or falsifying the origin, copyright or authorship notice.
- Misrepresenting origin, authorship or approval, or implying endorsement that
  the licensor did not give.

## 2. How to send a notice

Send notices to the copyright holder's designated address for legal correspondence.
Do not file enforcement notices in the public issue tracker, in a pull request, or
in a public discussion. Doing so tends to tip off the person you are reporting
before anything can be done about it.

A notice must be a complete written communication. It must include all of the
following. GitHub's own DMCA guidance is a good reference for the general shape of
these requirements.

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed. A link to
   the official repository or the canonical licence file is sufficient.
3. Identification of the infringing material, with a URL precise enough to locate
   it. A homepage is not enough. Link the specific file, release, or product page.
4. Your contact details: name, address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A good-faith statement: you have a good-faith belief that use of the material
   in the manner complained of is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent,
   or the law.
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is
   accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner, or an agent
   authorised to act on the owner's behalf, of the exclusive right that is
   infringed.
7. Your agreement to the jurisdiction of the courts of your district, and to the
   licensor's acceptance of jurisdiction, for the resolution of the dispute.

The full text of the 17 U.S.C. section 512(c)(3) requirements is reproduced at
<https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/512>. Substitute your own
jurisdiction where appropriate.

## 4. What happens after you send it

1. The notice is logged and a reference is assigned.
2. The infringing copy is assessed against the licence terms in section 1 above.
3. If the claim is valid, the material is taken down, or the claim is referred for
   legal advice if it is contested.
4. The person who posted the material is notified and given the opportunity to
   respond.
5. If they correct the problem, the material may be restored.

Infringing material may be removed without notice where the infringement is
obvious, such as a paid listing of the unmodified software with the origin notice
still intact on it.

## 5. Honest limits of this procedure

This section exists so that nothing here reads as a threat it is not.

- **Forks and GitHub Actions are not fully enforceable.** A public workflow can
  check a repository or a pull request. It cannot reach a copy already downloaded,
  a mirror on another host, a copy sold through a payment processor, or a
  redistribution that happened before the copy was indexed. Removing automated
  enforcement does not make a use lawful, but it does make it less likely to be
  noticed.
- **Terms cannot be changed retroactively.** A successor licence edition governs
  only from the point it is published. Copies already obtained keep the terms they
  were issued under. See Section 13 of the licence.
- **The signed commercial route is real.** A reseller, a host, or a bundle
  distributor can do all of this lawfully under a signed Commercial License
  Agreement. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md).
  A notice submitted by a party who holds such an agreement is not an infringement
  and will be closed.
- **A notice is not a legal finding.** It starts a process. It does not decide
  anything, and a false notice carries liability under 17 U.S.C. section 512(f).
- **This document is not legal advice.** If you need advice about your own position,
  take it from a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

## 6. Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed in error, you may send a counter-notice to the
same address containing:

1. Your signature.
2. Identification of the removed material and the location where it appeared.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the
   material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address, and telephone number.
5. A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the courts of your district and
   to the licensor's acceptance of jurisdiction.

The removed material may then be restored, unless the original complainant files a
court action.
